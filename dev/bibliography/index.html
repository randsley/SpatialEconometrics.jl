Bibliography

This page lists every work the package cites: in its code, its manual, its research notes and its roadmap. Each entry says where it is used.

  • Sections. The works the code or the manual use come first. Then come the works used only in the research notes (research/trace_cfe/), the works consulted for the roadmap, and the citations that do not yet have a checked entry.
  • Checks. Each entry was compared with one of three things: the publisher's record (through Scite or the DOI), the title page of the copy held in ExtRef/, or the reference in ROADMAP.md that records how it was checked. ROADMAP.md also records which parts of each work have been read.
  • Style. The entries are in APA style. A DOI is given where it was checked.
  • Quotes. A formula quoted from one of these works keeps the work's own symbols (see Notation).
