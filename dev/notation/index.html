Notation

The manual uses one set of symbols for the package's own text. This page lists them, then lists each page that departs from them, and why.

Quotes use the notation of their source

A theorem, formula or sentence quoted from a paper keeps that paper's words and symbols exactly, even where a symbol means something else in this manual. A line after the quote maps its symbols to the manual's. A page that implements one paper's model may also keep that paper's symbols throughout; the second table below lists those pages.

The core symbols

SymbolMeaningResult field or keyword
ythe outcome: an n-vector, or y_t / y_{it} in a panel
X, βthe regressors and their coefficientsbeta, coef
Wthe spatial weight matrix, n × n (N × N in a panel), zero diagonal
ρthe coefficient on the spatial lag Wyrho
λthe coefficient of the spatial error, u = λWu + εlambda
θthe coefficients on WX (SLX, SDM, SDEM)
ua disturbance before the spatial error filter
ε, σ²the innovations and their variancesigma2
ΣDiag(σᵢ²), the variances under heteroskedasticity
γ, ηin a dynamic panel: the coefficients on y_{t−1} and Wy_{t−1}gamma, eta
μᵢ, ξ_tunit and time effects
N, Tthe number of spatial units and of periodsthe argument N, T
nthe number of observations: n = N in a cross-section
i, j; tindices of units; of periods
I, 1_nthe identity; the vector of ones
kthe number of columns of X
Man annihilator I − P: residual-maker of a projection or a centring

Three symbols mean different things on different pages, by design:

  • λ: the spatial error coefficient (the core meaning), Li & Yang's lag coefficients, factor loadings, eigenvalues in spatial filtering, and the smoothing penalty of root_rho. Each page defines it where it uses it.
  • G: W(I − ρW)⁻¹ in the root estimator, R(λ)⁻¹W in the log-GARCH scores, and the network itself on the networks page.
  • Ω: the penalty of a smoother, MWᵤM in spatial filtering, and the middle of a sandwich variance (Ω̂).
